Eligibility and Documents

Requirements vary by lender, applicant profile, and the property being financed.

Common Eligibility Factors

  • Age and residency requirements set by the lender.
  • Income, employment, and financial stability.
  • Credit history and existing loan obligations.
  • Property value, documentation, and legal status.
  • Ability to make the required down payment.

Commonly Requested Documents

  • Identity proof and address proof.
  • Recent salary slips or business income records.
  • Bank statements and applicable tax returns.
  • Property documents and sale agreement, where applicable.
  • Additional documents requested by the lender.

This is a general guide, not a guarantee of eligibility or approval. The selected lender will confirm its specific requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more before beginning your application.

How much housing loan can I get?

The amount depends on the lender's policies, your income, existing obligations, credit profile, down payment, and property valuation.

Can I apply if I am self-employed?

Some lenders offer housing loans to eligible self-employed applicants. Income evidence, business records, and other requirements may apply.

How long does approval take?

Processing time varies by lender and depends on document verification, credit assessment, and property checks.

Does submitting an application guarantee approval?

No. The lender reviews the applicant's eligibility, documents, repayment capacity, and property before making a decision.
